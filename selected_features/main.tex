{\LARGE\bfseries Features used in the final BTP prediction model\par}
\vspace{4mm}
We extracted \textbf{60 baseline source features} for analysis. After comparing feature panels and models, the selected logistic regression model uses the following \textbf{28 core source features} to predict dementia conversion within five years.

\subsection*{Demographics and genetics 4 features}
\begingroup\small\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.22}\noindent\begin{tabular}{@{}p{.39\linewidth}p{.58\linewidth}@{}}\toprule\textbf{Feature column} & \textbf{Meaning}\\\midrule
\field{AGE} & Age at the baseline assessment.\\
\field{PTGENDER} & Recorded participant sex or gender category.\\
\field{PTEDUCAT} & Years of formal education.\\
\field{APOE4} & Number of APOE epsilon 4 alleles, recorded as 0, 1, or 2.\\
\bottomrule\end{tabular}\endgroup
\subsection*{Cognitive assessments 10 features}
\begingroup\small\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.22}\noindent\begin{tabular}{@{}p{.39\linewidth}p{.58\linewidth}@{}}\toprule\textbf{Feature column} & \textbf{Meaning}\\\midrule
\field{CDRSB_bl} & Clinical Dementia Rating sum of boxes.\\
\field{ADAS11_bl} & 11 item Alzheimer's Disease Assessment Scale cognitive score.\\
\field{ADAS13_bl} & 13 item Alzheimer's Disease Assessment Scale cognitive score.\\
\field{MMSE_bl} & Mini Mental State Examination total score.\\
\field{RAVLT_immediate_bl} & Rey Auditory Verbal Learning Test immediate recall score.\\
\field{RAVLT_learning_bl} & RAVLT learning score across repeated trials.\\
\field{RAVLT_forgetting_bl} & RAVLT forgetting score.\\
\field{RAVLT_perc_forgetting_bl} & RAVLT percentage forgetting score.\\
\field{FAQ_bl} & Functional Activities Questionnaire score.\\
\field{MOCA_bl} & Montreal Cognitive Assessment total score.\\
\bottomrule\end{tabular}\endgroup
